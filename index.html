Company background

Two companies, one mission: bringing advanced molecular imaging to Filipino patients.

KHealth

Founded in 2013, KHealth is the country's leading manufacturer of PET tracers, including FDG and FPSMA. KHealth is also the only commercial manufacturer of FBB (Neuraceq) and LuPSMA in the Philippines.

Careview

Established in 2017, Careview is an SEC-registered company that builds and operates Molecular Imaging Centers in the Philippines. It has established 8 PET/CT centers in Manila, Makati, Pampanga, and Batangas.

Mission and vision

The promises that guide our work every day.

Our mission

To provide and deliver the highest quality and cost-efficient radiopharmaceuticals and PET/CT imaging services, ensuring that all patients have access to diagnostic and therapeutic advancements with the best possible care.

Our vision

To be the country's leading and most trusted provider of innovative radiopharmaceuticals, cutting-edge PET/CT imaging services, and comprehensive healthcare solutions, which aims for excellence and patient-focused care by 2040.
